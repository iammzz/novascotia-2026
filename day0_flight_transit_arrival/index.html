    🎒 Field Notes

    • Travel documents: passport/ID and boarding passes (digital or printed).
    • Maritime Museum is a walk-in — open 9:30AM–4:30PM (Fridays in October), no evening admission. Aim to arrive by ~2:00PM for a full visit.
    • Layers: maritime winds swing 8–16°C in October — a windproof shell is worth carrying for the evening boardwalk.
    • Head start: Saturday is the long scenic Marine Drive to West Bay — sleep well and pack the night before.
