    ❓ Open Decision — Baddeck & the Alexander Graham Bell NHS (pencilled, not booked)

    Status: UNDECIDED as of Oct 9, 2026. Day 4 is the only day this route passes Baddeck, so it is the single window for the Bell museum. The museum sits 2 km off Hwy 105 Exit 9 — zero detour distance, but a real time cost — and this day has no slack. Pick one:

    Option A — Museum, 8:00 AM rollout (gives up the Grohmann factory floor)

    Time Stop Drive
    8:00–9:27AM Keltic → Alexander Graham Bell NHS, Baddeck 87 km
    9:30–11:45AM Museum: 2 h tour + rooftop viewing deck —
    11:45AM–2:30PM → Pictou 213 km
    2:30–3:30PM Grohmann Knives — showroom only (factory floor winds down ~3:00 PM) —
    3:30–4:15PM Hector Heritage Quay 1 km
    4:15–4:45PM Water Street treats (Acropole / Mrs. MacGregor's) —
    4:45–8:15PM → LaHave dome (lockbox check-in ~8:15 PM) 266 km

    Option A route in Google Maps →

    Option B — No museum (the schedule below, ← current default): 9:00 AM rollout, Grohmann's factory floor protected, dome ~7:30 PM. Baddeck becomes at most a 30–45 min waterfront leg-stretch (The Freight Shed, The Rusty Anchor, or the museum's rooftop deck and Tetra Café).

    Not recommended — "have both": a 9:00 AM rollout and the museum lands Pictou ~3:30 PM, which kills the factory floor anyway and costs Hector Quay + the Water Street treats. Same museum cost, less Pictou.

    Museum facts (verified at parks.canada.ca, Oct 9, 2026): open 7 days a week, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM through Oct 30, 2026 (no seasonal-closure risk on Oct 13) · $10.00 adult / $8.50 senior, not covered by the Cape Breton park daily admission · 559 Chebucto Street, Baddeck · Parks Canada's own guidance: "plan to spend at least two hours" · no advance booking needed.

    Recommendation: Option B unless the Bell museum is a genuine priority. If it is, take Option A and accept the showroom-only Grohmann stop.

    🚗 Transit · 🥾 Activity · 🍽️ Meal · ⚡ Quick stop

    Time Type Stop Drive What to do — and what to know

    7:30–10:30PM 🍽️ Private hot tub under the stars — Unwind in your private outdoor hot tub overlooking the water after the long drive. Dinner nearby on the South Shore, or artisan provisions picked up in Bridgewater / Lunenburg.

    🎒 Field Notes

    • ⚠️ Timing correction (Oct 9, 2026): this day used to claim a 10:30 AM rollout, "~6 h" of driving and a 2:00 PM Pictou arrival. Verified free-flow routing (OpenStreetMap/OSRM) gives Keltic → Pictou 297 km / 4 h 05 m (was listed 3 h 30 m) and Pictou → LaHave 266 km / 3 h 27 m (was listed 2 h 45 m) — a realistic day total of ~7 h 30 m driving, not ~6 h. The old timeline was impossible as written; a 10:30 AM rollout would have landed the dome at ~8:30 PM with the Grohmann factory floor long closed. Fixed by moving the rollout to 9:00 AM, which protects both the factory floor (~3:00 PM wind-down) and the ~7:30 PM dome check-in.
    • ⚠️ If you want a slow morning instead: a 10:00 AM rollout still works — Grohmann ~2:20 PM (floor caught with ~40 min to spare) — but the dome check-in slides to ~8:15 PM and the hot-tub evening shrinks. Do not push past 10:15 AM.
    • ❓ Baddeck is the open decision — see the top of this page. It is the only pass through Baddeck on the whole trip; it cannot be moved to another day.
    • ✈️ Airline baggage warning (Grohmann knives): CATSA/Transport Canada prohibit knives of any blade length in carry-on. If you buy at the factory outlet you must use checked baggage.
      • Option A (recommended) — ship from the factory: Grohmann packs and ships worldwide; ask them to send your purchase to Toronto (~$15–25 domestic, no customs). Avoids the WS811 checked-bag fee entirely.
      • Option B — self-ship from Halifax: sheath/tape blades, sturdy box, Canada Post Expedited Parcel with tracking + insurance (~$15–30).
      • Option C — add a checked bag to WS811 via Manage Trips (~$35–50).
    • Hot tub & dome: bring swimwear and flip-flops for the outdoor private hot tub.
    • Coastal layers: windproof jacket for Pictou's harbourfront and the South Shore evening breeze.
    • Fuel & food gaps: top up before the long 266 km run to LaHave (New Glasgow/Truro); after ~6PM the South Shore villages thin out — buy dinner provisions in Bridgewater or Lunenburg if you skip a restaurant.
