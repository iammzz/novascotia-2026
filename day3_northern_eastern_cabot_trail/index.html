    🕘 The Day, Stop by Stop

    🚗 Transit · 🥾 Activity · 🍽️ Meal · ⚡ Quick stop

    Time Type Stop Drive What to do — and what to know
    8:30–9:45AM 🚗 Check out of Chéticamp → alpine crossing 42 km Checkout by 10:00AM. Drive east over MacKenzie Mountain and North Mountain on the Cabot Trail (NS-30) — the most alpine stretch of the island.
    9:45–10:30AM ⚡ MacKenzie & North Mountain lookouts — Pull off at the MacKenzie Mountain Lookout and North Mountain Summit (445 m / 1,460 ft) for canyon views over the Big Intervale and Aspy River Valley in full autumn colour.
    10:30AM–12:45PM 🥾 Meat Cove — the edge of the island 30 km Cape Breton's wildest settlement. Take the rugged coastal road (final 8 km well-maintained gravel) to Meat Cove (47.0264° N, 60.5593° W). Walk the ~1.5 km cliffside trail, watch waves pound the sea stacks, and have lunch at the cliff-edge Chowder Hut. Grassy promontories drop ~150 m straight into the North Atlantic. Meat Cove guide →
    12:45–1:30PM 🚗 Drive back to the Cabot Trail 35 km Return south to rejoin the Cabot Trail at South Harbour / Dingwall.
    1:45–2:45PM 🍽️ Neils Harbour Lighthouse & lobster shack 35 km 1899 wooden lighthouse on the headland (46.8122° N, 60.3236° W), stacked lobster traps, and a working slipway. Sample hot chowder or ice cream right on the wharf.
    3:00–4:30PM 🥾 Middle Head hike (recommended) 20 km Middle Head: 3.8 km out-and-back, 85 m gain, 1.5–2 h — a narrow peninsula between the two Ingonish bays with grey seals and seabirds, right at the Keltic doorstep. Parks Canada →
    If you skip Cape Smokey or Meat Cove, swap in Franey Mountain instead (7.4 km loop, 365 m gain, 2.5–3 h, hard). Parks Canada →
    4:45–6:00PM ⚡ Cape Smokey Gondola 12 km Atlantic Canada's only gondola (38539 Cabot Trail). Last ride up 5:30PM, last ride down 6:00PM (from Oct 9) — arrive by ~4:45PM. 8-minute ride, then walk the summit timber skywalk over Ingonish Harbour. Skip if behind schedule or foggy. Cape Smokey →
    6:15–7:30PM 🏨 Check in at the Keltic Resort 12 km Drive back north to Keltic Resort, Ingonish Beach (Res. #27835, check-in from 4:00PM) and settle in.
    7:30–9:00PM 🍽️ Thanksgiving dinner at the Keltic Resort — Traditional Thanksgiving harvest dinner or seafood at the Purple Thistle Dining Room — book ahead (holiday Monday set menu). Then an easy walk down Middle Head for seals and sunset if energy allows.

    🎒 Field Notes

    • Sturdy hiking boots: Franey's rocky, rooted terrain and damp autumn sections demand grip.
    • Trekking poles: highly recommended for the steep 365 m Franey descent to protect knees.
    • Binoculars: for minke/pilot whales off Meat Cove and Middle Head, plus bald eagles along the cliffs.
    • Day 3 is the most ambitious day — Meat Cove + Neils Harbour + a hike + the gondola is a full schedule. If you fall behind, cut Cape Smokey or Meat Cove, not the Keltic check-in.
    • Cape Smokey gondola hours: from Oct 9 the last ride up is 5:30PM and last ride down 6:00PM — don't plan a post-6PM ride.
    • Seasonal closures: Meat Cove and Neils Harbour food vendors often close for the season right after Thanksgiving — call ahead or carry a backup snack.
    • Thanksgiving Monday: some attractions run reduced holiday hours; reserve Keltic's Thanksgiving dinner in advance.
