    🕘 The Day, Stop by Stop

    🚗 Transit · 🥾 Activity · 🍽️ Meal · ⚡ Quick stop

    Time Type Stop Drive What to do — and what to know
    8:15–9:45AM 🚗 Depart West Bay 95 km Check out of The Shoreline (checkout is 10:00AM, so an early start is easy) and head west on West Bay Rd → NS-105. Fill the tank in Whycocomagh — stations get sparse once you leave the Trans-Canada. Then turn onto the Cabot Trail (NS-19) at the Margaree junction.
    9:45–10:30AM 🥾 Margaree Valley — The Cabot Trail's warm-up act: crimson-and-gold maple ridges above the Southwest and Northeast Margaree salmon rivers. Stop at the Margaree Salmon Museum and pull into the riverside lookouts to watch fly-anglers. ~45 min of wandering. Margaree guide →
    10:30–11:15AM 🚗 Drive into Chéticamp 40 km The road bends north into Atlantic Canada's Acadian capital — watch for the painted Acadian flag and the working lobster fleet.
    11:15AM–12:30PM 🥾 Chéticamp & Les Trois Pignons — Les Trois Pignons (15584 Cabot Trail) is the world-renowned Acadian hooked-rug museum, with live hooking demonstrations (~45 min; site →). Then take a short harbourfront walk among the boats.
    12:30–1:30PM 🍽️ Lunch + Aucoin Bakery — Acadian seafood lunch in town — L'Abri or Harbour Restaurant (see Eat & Drink below). Afterward, grab road snacks at La Boulangerie Aucoin (since 1955): meat pies (pâté à la viande), cinnamon buns, and cheddar bread.
    1:30–1:45PM ⚡ Park gate & Visitor Centre 5 km Buy daily admission at the gate (~$21/day for 2 adults — cheaper than a $145 Discovery Pass for two park days), grab a park map, and read the latest moose and bear advisory. Cell service effectively ends here — download your offline maps now.
    1:45–3:15PM ⚡ French Mountain & Cap Rouge Lookouts 15 km Climb the legendary switchbacks 450 m straight out of the sea. Pull over at Cap Rouge Lookout and the French Mountain summit for cliff-edge Gulf of St. Lawrence panoramas. (On the way back down later, use engine braking — shift to 2nd/3rd — to save the brakes.)
    3:15–3:50PM 🚗 Drive to Skyline Trailhead 20 km Continue up the Cabot Trail into the highlands. The lot has interpretive panels and pit toilets (46.7410° N, 60.8804° W).
    3:50–6:30PM 🥾 Skyline Trail sunset hike — Your 4:00PM reservation has an arrival window of 3:50–5:00PM — save the Parks Canada confirmation offline. 6.5 km round trip, ~145 m gain, moderate. ~1 h out through boreal forest and blueberry scrub — prime moose country; keep at least 30 m distance. Then 287 boardwalk steps down to the headland. Sunset over the Gulf is around 6:35PM. (Receipt: receipts/skyline.pdf) Parks Canada → · AllTrails →
    6:30–7:30PM 🥾 Headlamp return — The forest section is genuinely dark — headlamps are mandatory (300+ lumens). The exposed plateau can feel near 4°C at dusk, so have the windproof shell and beanie handy.
    7:30–8:00PM 🚗 Check in at the Chéticamp suite 25 km Drive back down to The Sunset Harbour Village Suite (15294 Cabot Trail A; self-check-in details come from host Joeleen). Dropping your bags here — 20 minutes from the trailhead — is the whole point: no 2-hour night drive through moose country.
    8:00–9:30PM 🍽️ Dinner at L'Abri — A celebratory Acadian feast: fresh Gulf seafood, fricot, and craft cocktails. Call ahead on the holiday weekend to confirm evening hours.

    🎒 Field Notes

    • Headlamp (mandatory for the dark forest return) and spare batteries.
    • Windproof shell + warm beanie — the headland is fully exposed to maritime gales.
    • Binoculars for moose on the plateau and whales/eagles off the headland.
    • Offline maps + Skyline confirmation saved before entering the park.
    • Moose rule: never approach; keep 30 m, and never stand between a cow and calf.
