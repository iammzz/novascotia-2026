## 💡 3. "Tips" (Essential Travel Advice, Road Rules & Field Reminders)

> *Critical practical knowledge gathered for Cape Breton driving, ocean safety, transit tolls, local food traditions, and airline regulations. See [Travel Tips Guide](tips.md) for full context.*

### 🦌 Wildlife & Mountain Driving Safety
- **Moose Collision Hazard in Cape Breton Highlands**:
  - The national park is home to ~1,800 Eastern Canadian Moose. Mature bulls weigh up to 600 kg (1,300 lbs) with high centers of gravity, causing them to crush car windshields upon impact.
  - Moose are dark-coated and their eyes **do not reflect headlight beams** like deer.
  - Peak activity occurs at dusk, between sunset and 10:00 PM, and around dawn.
  - **Golden Rule**: **Avoid high-speed night driving** between Chéticamp and Ingonish. Continuous scan of road shoulders and tree lines; use high beams whenever oncoming traffic allows.
- **Engine Braking on Steep Mountain Grades**:
  - Descending French Mountain (13% grade) and North Mountain will overheat and glaze standard rental brakes.
  - Shift your transmission into Low / Sport manual mode (2nd or 3rd gear) to let engine compression control your downhill speed.
- **Scenic Lookout Etiquette**:
  - Cabot Trail roads are winding and narrow. If faster local drivers or trucks appear in your rearview mirror, pull into the next paved scenic overlook to let them pass.
- **Northern Loop Gas Strategy**:
  - Service stations are sparse between Chéticamp, Cape North, and Ingonish. Never enter the highland interior with less than half a tank; top off in Baddeck, Chéticamp, or Ingonish.

### 🌊 Ocean & Coastal Safety
- **The Peggy's Cove "Black Rocks" Life-or-Death Rule**:
  - **NEVER step onto wet, dark, or seaweed-covered granite rocks.** Dark rock indicates the splash zone of rogue Atlantic swell waves.
  - Freezing water temperatures, heavy underwater undertows, and sheer slippery rock faces make self-rescue virtually impossible. Always stay high on dry, pale white granite or wooden viewing decks.
- **Bay of Fundy Tidal Awareness**:
  - If stopping at tidal basins or mudflats, remember tides rise up to 16 meters at speeds up to 15 km/h. Return to high ground at least **2 hours before high tide**.

### 📱 Connectivity & Navigation Tips
- **Cellular Dead Zones**:
  - Reliable cell service drops out on the northern Cabot Trail between Pleasant Bay, Cape North, and Dingwall.
  - **Action**: Download Google Maps offline areas for Cape Breton Island and Halifax before leaving Toronto. Save trail maps on AllTrails / Maps.me.

### 🔪 Luggage, Transit & Financial Tips
- **Grohmann Knives Airport Security (CATSA)**:
  - Fixed blades and folding knives are 100% prohibited in carry-on cabin baggage at Halifax Stanfield (YHZ).
  - All knives must be shipped home from Pictou or packed securely inside checked baggage. Keep them in their protective sleeves and original factory boxes wrapped within soft apparel.
- **LaHave Cable Ferry**:
  - Crossing the LaHave River between East LaHave and LaHave saves driving all the way around Bridgewater. Toll is **$7.00 CAD cash per vehicle** (5-minute crossing, departs every 15–30 minutes).
- **Halifax Harbour Bridge Tolls**:
  - The Angus L. Macdonald and A. Murray MacKay bridges connecting Halifax and Dartmouth charge a **$1.25 CAD toll**.
  - Keep loose Canadian loonies ($1) and quarters ($0.25) in the car cup holder if your rental transponder is not activated.
- **Halifax Passenger Ferry**:
  - Connecting Cable Wharf (Halifax) to Alderney Landing (Dartmouth) costs just **$2.75 CAD** cash and offers unmatched harbour skyline views.
- **Rental Car Refueling**:
  - Before returning your vehicle at YHZ airport, refuel at standard-rate gas stations along Highway 102 (Exit 7 in Enfield or Elmsdale) rather than paying the inflated on-airport markup.
- **Dining Tipping**:
  - Standard tipping in Nova Scotia hospitality venues is 15% to 20%.

### 🦞 Local Culinary Etiquette
- **LaHave Bakery Experience**:
  - Located at 3421 Hwy 331 (2 minutes from the dome). Famous for house-baked artisan breads, cheddar dill scones, espresso, and craft shops upstairs. Perfect morning pitstop before taking the ferry to Lunenburg!
- **Traditional Atlantic Lobster Suppers**:
  - Bibs are provided and expected—wear one without hesitation.
  - Pull claws first, crack with metal cracker, break off tail, and push the meat out with a fork.
  - The rich green substance inside the body is *tomalley* (lobster liver)—esteemed as a local delicacy.
- **Pictou County Brown Sauce Pizza**:
  - A regional phenomenon distinct from Italian red marinara. The thick sauce is slow-cooked with celery, onions, chicken broth, and Greek spices, topped with Halifax-made spicy Brothers pepperoni. Grab a slice at *Acropole Pizza* in Pictou right next to the Grohmann factory.
