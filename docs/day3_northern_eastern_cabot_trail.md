## 🕘 The Day, Stop by Stop

**🚗 Transit · 🥾 Activity · 🍽️ Meal · ⚡ Quick stop**

| Time | Type | Stop | Drive | What to do — and what to know |
|:---:|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| **8:30–9:45AM** | 🚗 | **Check out of Chéticamp → alpine crossing** | 42 km | Checkout by 10:00AM. Drive east over **MacKenzie Mountain** and **North Mountain** on the Cabot Trail (NS-30) — the most alpine stretch of the island. |
| **9:45–10:30AM** | ⚡ | **MacKenzie & North Mountain lookouts** | — | Pull off at the **MacKenzie Mountain Lookout** and **North Mountain Summit** (445 m / 1,460 ft) for canyon views over the Big Intervale and Aspy River Valley in full autumn colour. |
| **10:30AM–12:45PM** | 🥾 | **Meat Cove — the edge of the island** | 30 km | Cape Breton's wildest settlement. Take the rugged coastal road (final 8 km well-maintained gravel) to **Meat Cove** (47.0264° N, 60.5593° W). Walk the ~1.5 km cliffside trail, watch waves pound the sea stacks, and have lunch at the cliff-edge **Chowder Hut**. Grassy promontories drop ~150 m straight into the North Atlantic. [Meat Cove guide →](https://www.novascotia.com/places-to-go/regions/cape-breton/meat-cove) |
| **12:45–1:30PM** | 🚗 | **Drive back to the Cabot Trail** | 35 km | Return south to rejoin the Cabot Trail at South Harbour / Dingwall. |
| **1:45–2:45PM** | 🍽️ | **Neils Harbour Lighthouse & lobster shack** | 35 km | 1899 wooden lighthouse on the headland (46.8122° N, 60.3236° W), stacked lobster traps, and a working slipway. Sample hot chowder or ice cream right on the wharf. |
| **3:00–6:00PM** | 🥾 | **Ingonish hike — choose your adventure** | 20 km | **Option 1 · Franey Mountain:** 7.4 km loop, 365 m gain, 2.5–3 h, hard switchback climb to a summit platform over the Clyburn Brook canyon. [Parks Canada →](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ns/cbreton/activ/randonnee-hiking/franey) <br>**Option 2 · Middle Head:** 3.8 km out-and-back, 85 m gain, 1.5–2 h, a narrow peninsula between the two Ingonish bays with grey seals and seabirds. [Parks Canada →](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ns/cbreton/activ/randonnee-hiking/middle-head) |
| **6:15–7:15PM** | ⚡ | **Cape Smokey Gondola** | 12 km | Atlantic Canada's only gondola (38539 Cabot Trail). 8-minute ride, then walk the summit timber skywalk looking south across Ingonish Harbour and Smokey Mountain Headland. [Cape Smokey →](https://capesmokey.ca/) |
| **7:30–9:30PM** | 🍽️ | **Thanksgiving dinner at the Keltic Resort** | 8 km | Settle into the **Keltic Resort** (Res. #27835). Traditional Thanksgiving harvest dinner or seafood at the **Purple Thistle Dining Room**, then an easy walk down **Middle Head** for seals and sunset if energy allows. |

## 🎒 Field Notes

- **Sturdy hiking boots**: Franey's rocky, rooted terrain and damp autumn sections demand grip.
- **Trekking poles**: highly recommended for the steep 365 m Franey descent to protect knees.
- **Binoculars**: for minke/pilot whales off Meat Cove and Middle Head, plus bald eagles along the cliffs.
