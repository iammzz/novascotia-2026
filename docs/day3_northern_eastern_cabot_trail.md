## 🎒 Field Notes

- **Sturdy hiking boots**: Franey's rocky, rooted terrain and damp autumn sections demand grip.
- **Trekking poles**: highly recommended for the steep 365 m Franey descent to protect knees.
- **Binoculars**: for minke/pilot whales off Meat Cove and Middle Head, plus bald eagles along the cliffs.
- **Day 3 is the most ambitious day** — Meat Cove + Neils Harbour + a hike + the gondola is a full schedule. If you fall behind, cut Cape Smokey or Meat Cove, **not** the Keltic check-in.
- **Cape Smokey gondola hours**: from Oct 9 the last ride up is **5:30PM** and last ride down **6:00PM** — don't plan a post-6PM ride.
- **Seasonal closures**: Meat Cove and Neils Harbour food vendors often close for the season right after Thanksgiving — call ahead or carry a backup snack.
- **Thanksgiving Monday**: some attractions run reduced holiday hours; reserve Keltic's Thanksgiving dinner in advance.
