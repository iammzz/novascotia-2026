## 🥾 Module 3: Active Adventure & Hiking Intensity

### Question 3.1: Beyond the mandatory Skyline Trail sunset hike, what intensity level do you want for your daily hikes?

- [ ] **Level 1: High Scenic Reward / Moderate Exertion (5–8 km / day)**
  - **Skyline Trail**: 6.5 km gentle headland boardwalk loop + sunset viewing & moose spotting.
  - **Middle Head Trail**: 3.8 km ocean peninsula hike between North and South Ingonish bays.
  - **Uisge Bàn Falls**: 2.7 km serene river gorge walk to a 16m waterfall in fall foliage.

- [ ] **Level 2: Active & Challenging Peaks (8–14 km / day)**

  - Adds **Cape Split Trail** (13.2 km coastal forest trek to 200-foot sea stacks over Bay of Fundy tidal whirlpools).
- [ ] **Level 3: Full-Day Rugged Wilderness Challenge**
  - Adds **Pollett's Cove Trail** (16 km round-trip strenuous cliff-edge trek to the secret valley where wild horses graze by the ocean).

## 🌊 Module 4: High-Adrenaline & Unique Coastal Novelty

### Question 4.1: Which of these signature Nova Scotia bucket-list adventures excite you most?

- [ ] **Tidal Bore Rafting (Shubenacadie River)**:
  - *What it is*: Ride 10-to-14 foot standing mud waves in a high-powered motorized Zodiac as 160 billion tonnes of Bay of Fundy tide surges upriver. Includes mud-sliding down natural riverbanks in full rain gear! (Operates through Thanksgiving weekend).
- [ ] **Ocean Floor Walking & Mudflat Exploration (Burntcoat Head Park)**:
  - *What it is*: Walk on the literal bottom of the ocean where the highest tides on Earth (up to 53 vertical feet) drop away to reveal towering sea stacks and marine life.
- [ ] **Atlantic Autumn Coastal Surfing (Lawrencetown Beach)**:
  - *What it is*: Nova Scotia is Canada's premier cold-water surfing hub with consistent autumn hurricane swells. Rent 5/4mm wetsuits and boards or take a private 2-person lesson.
- [ ] **Zodiac Whale Watching (Pleasant Bay / Ingonish)**:
  - *What it is*: High-speed Zodiac charter spotting pilot whales, minke whales, humpbacks, and dolphins along the Cabot Trail cliffs.

### Question 6.1: What dining experiences are non-negotiable for you two?

- [ ] **The Classic Multi-Course Lobster Supper**: All-you-can-eat steamed mussels, seafood chowder, whole freshly steamed Atlantic lobster with drawn butter, and homemade pie (*Baddeck Lobster Suppers* or *Shore Club*).
- [ ] **Modern Atlantic Fine Dining & Raw Oyster Bar**: Hyper-seasonal local seafood, handmade pasta, and craft cocktails (*Bar Kismet* — Top 15 in Canada, *The Bicycle Thief*, *Drift* at Queen's Marque).

- [ ] **Single Malt Whisky Tour at Glenora Distillery**: North America's first single malt distillery in Mabou, tasting "Glen Breton Rare" aged in oak barrels.
- [ ] **Halifax Street Food & Historic Brewery**: Original Halifax Donairs from *King of Donair (KOD)* and the theatrical 1820 *Alexander Keith's Historic Brewery* tour.
- [x] **Pictou County Pizza & Grohmann Knives**: Sample the legendary spicy brown-sauce pizza at *Acropole Pizza* on Water Street in Pictou and tour the *Grohmann Knives Factory & Outlet* to shop for handcrafted Canadian belt knives.
