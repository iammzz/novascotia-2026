    🕘 The Day, Stop by Stop

    ✈️ Flight · 🚗 Transit · 🥾 Activity · 🍽️ Meal · ⚡ Quick stop

    Time Type Stop Drive What to do — and what to know
    8:30–9:30AM 🍽️ LaHave Bakery 2 km Check out of The Lookout Dome One (checkout by 11:00AM, but a ~9:00AM departure maximizes the day). Grab cheddar-dill scones, sourdough, and espresso at LaHave Bakery (3421 Hwy 331) right over the water.
    9:30–10:00AM 🚗 LaHave Cable Ferry 2 km Historic cable ferry, every 15–30 min, $7.00 CAD cash. 5-minute crossing across the LaHave River mouth to East LaHave, connecting to Route 332 North toward Lunenburg.
    10:00AM–12:30PM 🥾 Lunenburg Old Town (UNESCO) 15 km One of only two UNESCO World Heritage towns in North America. Walk the colourful 18th-century captain's homes along King and Pelham Streets and the working Montague Street waterfront. View the Bluenose II berth and the Fisheries Museum of the Atlantic, then an early farewell seafood lunch on the water. Lunenburg → · Fisheries Museum →
    12:45–2:00PM 🥾 Mahone Bay — Three Churches 10 km The postcard view of the Three Churches (St. James Anglican, St. John's Evangelical Lutheran, Trinity United) reflected in the harbour. Stroll Edgewater Street and Amos Pewter for handcrafted pewter and local pottery. Mahone Bay → · Amos Pewter →
    2:00–3:10PM 🚗 Drive to Peggy's Cove 80 km North on Hwy 103 to Exit 5, then Route 333 (Prospect Road) around St. Margaret's Bay (~1h 10m).
    3:10–4:00PM 🥾 Peggy's Point Lighthouse — 72 Peggy's Point Rd (44.4930° N, 63.9181° W). Walk the pale granite headland and the accessible viewing platform for the red-and-white beacon against the surf. ⚠️ STAY OFF THE BLACK ROCKS — rogue swells surge over dark rock; stay on high, dry white granite. Grab warm gingerbread in lemon sauce at the Sou'Wester. Peggy's Cove →
    4:00–5:30PM 🚗 Scenic run to YHZ 70 km Depart by 4:00PM sharp via Route 333 North → Hwy 102 North (~50 min driving). Aim to arrive ~5:15–5:30PM, a full 2h 45m before departure.
    5:30–6:15PM ⚡ Refuel, return car, check bag — Fuel on Hwy 102 (not the pricier airport station), return the National rental (#2098647349) at the YHZ garage, and check the bag (Grohmann knives — never carry-on). (Receipt: receipts/Confirmation _ National Car Rental.pdf)
    6:15–8:15PM 🍽️ Security, last coffee, gate — Clear security and relax before boarding.
    8:15–9:45PM ✈️ WestJet WS811 → Toronto — Non-stop Boeing 737 MAX 8, 2h 30m. Land 9:45PM ET — late night home, plan transit/parking ahead.

    🎒 Field Notes

    • 🚨 Add a checked bag to WS811 NOW (WestJet Manage Trips): UltraBasic has no carry-on and no checked bag — the Grohmann knives and wine are prohibited in carry-on.
    • Comfortable walking shoes: Lunenburg Old Town is walkable but hilly and cobbled.
    • Camera: Peggy's lighthouse, the three churches, and candy-coloured Lunenburg streets.
    • Car packed all day: you check out in the morning and won't return — load everything at checkout; the only stop is YHZ.
    • Rental return kit: National confirmation (#2098647349) + phone charging cable for offline maps on Hwy 103 (patchy in places).
    • Late night home: landing 9:45PM ET means a Toronto front door around 11:30PM — line up transit/parking ahead.
